Nothing here is medical advice

This website describes the care we provide. It is not medical, nursing, psychiatric or legal advice about any individual, and it must not be used to decide anything about a particular person's treatment or medication.

Care needs are assessed individually, by qualified staff, before and during a stay. Two people with the same diagnosis often need very different care. If you are worried about someone's health, speak to a doctor.

Dementia information

Where we describe dementia or Alzheimer's disease, we do so to explain how we care for people living with them. It is general information, not a diagnostic tool, and it is no substitute for assessment by a specialist.
