Photographs

The place, as it actually looks

These are our own residents, our own staff and our own gardens. Nobody here is a model, and none of these are stock photographs. Some were taken several years ago, so gardens have grown and buildings have been repainted since. The only reliable way to judge the place is to walk around it.

The grounds

Twelve acres, more than 1,000 trees, a lake and fishponds.

The villas

58 villa rooms, in modern Thai style, each with its own terrace.

Pools, fitness and spa

Two pools, one with ramp access and a lift into the water.

Care

Nursing on site, 24 hours.

Daily life

Activities run seven days a week. You join the ones you feel like.

Memory care

Dementia care inside the same gardens as everyone else, not behind a separate door.

About these photographs

Why there are not more of them, and why some are soft

We would rather show you a small number of real photographs than a large number of stock ones. The people in these images live and work here.

Some of them are old and were taken on whatever camera was to hand, which is why a few look soft on a large screen. We have chosen not to hide that behind filters or heavy cropping. Facilities, planting and staff change over time, so treat these as representative rather than as a guarantee of how any particular villa looks today.
